## Wave basics and the wave equation (5.1–5.7)

**Q1 [F] Spec 5.3, 5.4 (4 marks)**

(a) State what is meant by the amplitude of a wave. (1)

(b) State what a wave transfers and what it does not transfer. (2)

(c) Give the unit of frequency. (1)

<details class="markscheme">
<summary>Show mark scheme</summary>

| (a) maximum displacement from the rest position | 1 |
| --- | --- |
| (b) transfers energy (and information) (1); does not transfer matter (1) | 2 |
| (c) hertz (Hz) | 1 |

***Examiner note:*** *Amplitude is from the rest position to a crest, not crest-to-trough.*

</details>

**Q2 [C] Spec 5.5, 5.6 (5 marks)**

(a) A wave has a frequency of 50 Hz and a wavelength of 0.080 m. Calculate its speed. (3)

(b) Calculate the period of the wave. (2)

<details class="markscheme">
<summary>Show mark scheme</summary>

| (a) v = f × λ (1); 50 × 0.080 (1); 4.0 m/s (1) | 3 |
| --- | --- |
| (b) T = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ 50 (1); 0.020 s (1) | 2 |

***Examiner note:*** *Write the formula first; it often earns a mark even if the arithmetic slips.*

</details>

## Total internal reflection (5.20–5.22)

**Q16 [E] Spec 5.22 (3 marks)**

Diamond has a refractive index of 2.42. (a) Calculate its critical angle. (2) (b) State what the small critical angle means for how a cut diamond looks. (1)

<details class="markscheme">
<summary>Show mark scheme</summary>

| (a) sin c = 1 ÷ 2.42 = 0.413 (1); c = 24.4° (1) | 2 |
| --- | --- |
| (b) light is totally internally reflected many times inside, so the diamond sparkles | 1 |

***Examiner note:*** *A larger refractive index gives a smaller critical angle, trapping light more easily.*

</details>

**Q24 [C] Spec 5.5, 5.6 (4 marks)**

A microphone shows a sound wave with a time period of 2.5 ms. (a) Calculate the frequency. (2) (b) If the speed of sound is 340 m/s, calculate the wavelength. (2)

<details class="markscheme">
<summary>Show mark scheme</summary>

| (a) f = 1 ÷ T = 1 ÷ 0.0025 (1); 400 Hz (1) | 2 |
| --- | --- |
| (b) λ = v ÷ f = 340 ÷ 400 (1); 0.85 m (1) | 2 |

***Examiner note:*** *Convert ms to s first: 2.5 ms = 0.0025 s.*

</details>
