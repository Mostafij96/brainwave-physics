## Efficiency & Sankey Diagrams

Spec: 3.4, 3.5

**Q6**  A filament lamp uses 60J of electrical energy per second. It produces 9J of useful light energy. Calculate (a) the efficiency as a decimal, (b) the efficiency as a percentage, (c) the energy wasted per second.   [4 marks]

**Q7 An electric motor is 72% efficient and produces 3600J of useful kinetic energy. Calculate the total electrical energy input. [3 marks]**

**Q8 Describe what is meant by a Sankey diagram. State what the WIDTH of each arrow represents. [3 marks]**

**Q9**  [Extended] A car engine receives 5000J of chemical energy per second. It produces 1500J of useful kinetic energy and 2000J of waste heat from the exhaust. The remainder is wasted as heat in the engine. (a) Calculate the energy wasted by the engine. (b) Calculate the efficiency of the car engine as a percentage. (c) Draw a Sankey diagram to represent these energy transfers.   [6 marks]

| **Q7** | Total = useful ÷ efficiency = 3600 ÷ 0.72 = 5000J (formula+sub+eval) | **3** |
