## Density

Spec: 4.1, 4.3, 4.4P

**Q2 Define density and state its SI unit. [2 marks]**

**Q3 A metal block has a mass of 1560 g and a volume of 200 cm³. Calculate its density in kg/m³. [3 marks]**

**Q4 Aluminium has a density of 2700 kg/m³. A component has a volume of 0.0004 m³. Calculate its mass. [2 marks]**

**Q5 Describe how you would measure the density of an irregularly shaped object such as a small rock. [4 marks]**

**Q6 Explain, in terms of density, why a hollow steel ship floats but a solid steel ball sinks. [3 marks]**

| **Q** | **Mark Points (compact)** | **M** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Q2** | Mass per unit volume (1); kg/m³ (1) | **2** |
| **Q3** | m=1.56kg (1); V=0.0002m³ (1); ρ=1.56/0.0002=7800 kg/m³ (1) | **3** |
| **Q4** | m=ρV=2700×0.0004=1.08 kg (formula + answer) | **2** |
| **Q5** | Fill measuring cylinder, record V₁ (1); submerge object, record V₂ (1); V=V₂−V₁ (1); measure mass, ρ=m/V (1) | **4** |
| **Q6** | Ship hollow → average density &lt; water (1+1); ball solid steel density > water (1); less dense floats, denser sinks | **3** |
