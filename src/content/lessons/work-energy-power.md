Energy is the ability to do work. It cannot be created or destroyed, only transferred from one store to another. This is the **law of conservation of energy**.

## Work done

Work is done when a force moves an object.

> **W = F × d**

- `W` = work done (joules, J)
- `F` = force (N)
- `d` = distance moved in the direction of the force (m)

**Example:** Pushing a box with 50 N over 4 m does 50 × 4 = **200 J** of work.

## Kinetic energy

Energy of a moving object.

> **KE = ½ m v²**

**Example:** A 2 kg ball moves at 3 m/s. KE = ½ × 2 × 3² = **9 J**.

## Gravitational potential energy

Energy stored because of height.

> **GPE = m g h**

**Example:** A 5 kg book lifted 2 m (g = 10 N/kg): GPE = 5 × 10 × 2 = **100 J**.

## Power

Power is the rate of doing work.

> **P = W ÷ t**

Power is measured in watts (W). 1 W = 1 J/s.

**Example:** A motor does 600 J of work in 12 s. P = 600 ÷ 12 = **50 W**.

## Efficiency

> **efficiency = (useful energy output ÷ total energy input) × 100%**

No machine is 100% efficient, because some energy is always wasted as heat or sound.
