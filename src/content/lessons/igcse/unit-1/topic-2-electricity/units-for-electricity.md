**2.1**  Use the following units: ampere (A), coulomb (C), joule (J), ohm (Ω), second (s), volt (V), watt (W)

| **Quantity** | **Unit** | **Symbol** | **Measured by** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Electric current | Ampere | **A** | Ammeter |
| Charge | Coulomb | **C** | Calculated |
| Energy | Joule | **J** | Calculated |
| Resistance | Ohm | **Ω** | Ohmmeter |
| Time | Second | **s** | Timer/clock |
| Voltage | Volt | **V** | Voltmeter |
| Power | Watt | **W** | Calculated |

<div class="callout callout-tip"><p class="callout-title">Key fact</p><p>1 ampere = 1 coulomb of charge flowing per second (1 A = 1 C/s)</p><p>⭐  1 volt = 1 joule per coulomb (1 V = 1 J/C) — this is the DEFINITION of the volt</p><p>⭐  1 watt = 1 joule per second (1 W = 1 J/s)</p><p>⭐  kWh (kilowatt-hour) is used for energy on electricity bills: 1 kWh = 3 600 000 J</p></div>
