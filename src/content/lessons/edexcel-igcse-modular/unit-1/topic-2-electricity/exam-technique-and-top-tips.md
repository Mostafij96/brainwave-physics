<div class="callout callout-tip"><p class="callout-title">Key fact</p><p>CALCULATIONS: Always write the formula first → substitute values → calculate → give units. Show every step.</p><p>⭐  DESCRIBE vs EXPLAIN: 'Describe' = say what happens. 'Explain' = say WHAT happens AND WHY.</p><p>⭐  FUSE: I = P ÷ V → then choose next fuse ABOVE that value. Never equal to it.</p><p>⭐  SERIES CHECK: V₁ + V₂ must equal V_supply. If not, recalculate.</p><p>⭐  STATIC: 'Electrons transfer' — never say 'protons transfer'. 'Object loses electrons → positive'.</p><p>⭐  POWER FORMULA CHOICE: P=IV (have I and V) · P=I²R (have I and R) · P=V²/R (have V and R).</p><p>⭐  LDR/THERMISTOR: Both decrease in resistance when stimulus increases (light/temperature).</p><p>⭐  PARALLEL: Voltage is SAME across each branch. Current ADDS UP at junctions.</p><p>⭐  FUSE POSITION: Always in series with the LIVE wire — never in the neutral wire.</p><p>⭐  PHOTOCOPIER: (−) toner attracted to (+) areas of drum — this is the key sentence for full marks.</p></div>

### Guaranteed Mark Questions — Topic 2

| **Question Type** | **Model Answer Structure** |
| --- | --- |
| **Why does a current heat a resistor?** | Electrons collide with positive metal ions (1) → energy transferred from electrons to ions (1) → ions vibrate more → temperature rises (1) |
| **Why are house lights in parallel?** | Each gets full supply voltage / stays at full brightness (1) · one failing doesn't affect others (1) · each can be switched independently (1) |
| **How is a plastic rod charged positively?** | Rubbed with cloth → electrons transferred FROM rod TO cloth (1) · rod LOSES electrons (1) · so rod has more protons than electrons → positive (1) |
| **Fuse selection for a 2300W kettle at 230V?** | I = P÷V = 2300÷230 = 10A (1) · choose next fuse above 10A → 13A fuse (1) |
| **How does a photocopier use static?** | Drum charged (+) all over (1) · light removes charge from white areas (1) · (−) toner attracted to remaining (+) areas (1) · paper picks up pattern (1) |
